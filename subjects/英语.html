长难句精析:过去分词作后置定语+介词with复合结构(真题句式) ​

掌握度: 50% | 创建: 2026-09-03 | 最近复习: 2026-09-03

掌握度进度: █████░░░░░

核心内容:

句式识别:①过去分词作后置定语(表被动):The oil pumped from the well ... = The oil which is pumped from the well(泵出的原油);单个过去分词置于名词前、分词短语置于名词后,被修饰名词是动作的承受者。②with+名词+宾补(介词with的复合结构)作状语:with the pressure maintained constantly(压力保持恒定),with the pump running(泵在运转),with the valve closed(阀门关闭)。③翻译技巧:分词短语先译成'被…的…'定语前置,或拆成并列句。考研英语一阅读/翻译真题高频结构,化工英语同样常见。例句:FCC units, equipped with regenerators and operated at high temperatures, convert heavy oil into lighter products, with the catalyst circulating continuously. 主干:FCC units convert heavy oil into lighter products;两个过去分词短语 equipped with regenerators / operated at high temperatures 作后置定语修饰 FCC units;with the catalyst circulating continuously 为独立结构状语(表伴随)。译文:催化裂化装置配备再生器并在高温下运行,将重油转化为较轻的产品,催化剂在其中持续循环。

仿写识别题:Refineries, ___ with advanced control systems and ___ around the clock, process crude oil into fuels, with the temperature ___ within a narrow range. A) equipping / operated / maintained B) equipped / operating / maintaining C) equipped / operated / maintained D) equipped / operated / maintaining → 答案 C:过去分词表被动(被配备/被操作),with+名词+过去分词表被动(温度被维持在)。验算:equipped with 表被动正确;operated 与 equipped 并列修饰 refineries;with the temperature maintained within a narrow range 独立结构,温度是被维持 → maintained。考点:分词作定语主动ing/被动ed的区分。
